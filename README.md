# Computer Networks: hands-on labs in Packet Tracer

Six labs in Cisco Packet Tracer, from your first switch to VLANs, routing, SSH and port security. Open `index.html`, or use the published site.

Each lab has three pages:

- **Learn:** the ideas, the network to build, every step with its commands and screenshots, a small tool to try, and the Packet Tracer file with its configuration scripts.
- **Exercises:** six hands-on tasks from easy to hard, each with a hint and a full solution: the topology, the cables and what to do on each device.
- **Test yourself:** 12 multiple-choice questions with explained answers. Your best score stays in your own browser.

You need [Cisco Packet Tracer](https://www.netacad.com), free with a Cisco Networking Academy account. The labs were built and tested in version 9.0.1.
